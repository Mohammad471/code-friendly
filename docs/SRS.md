# Software Requirement Specification (SRS)

# Project Name

Code-Friendly

---

## Vision

To provide engineering students with a structured and engaging learning platform that guides them from beginner to industry-ready by offering skill roadmaps, topic-wise learning, Smart Compare Mode, practice, quizzes, and gamified progress tracking.

---

## Problem Statement

Students often don't know what to learn or where to begin. They waste time searching for resources and following random tutorials.

Code-Friendly solves this by providing a structured roadmap and guided learning experience.

---

## Target Users

- B.Tech Students
- Diploma Students (Future)
- MCA Students (Future)

---

## User Roles

### Learner

- Register/Login
- Select Skills
- Learn Topics
- Solve Practice Questions
- Take Quizzes
- Earn Badges
- Track Progress

### Admin (Future)

- Manage Skills
- Manage Topics
- Manage Quizzes
- Manage Badges
